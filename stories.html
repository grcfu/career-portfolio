Thought Leadership 15 pts

Core Story Set

Four behavioral stories in STAR format — the ones I would actually tell in an interview.

What you need to do
  • Pick 4 of the prompts. Four story blocks are stubbed below — swap the prompt text on each.
  • 100–150 words each. Result is the part everyone rushes. Put a number in it if you can.
  • Options: leadership · initiative · teamwork · working with data · managing conflict · persuading someone · giving a presentation
  • Spread them across different experiences. Four stories about the same club is one story.
