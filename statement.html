What a software engineer actually does all day, and why I want to be one.

Note This must be a different field from the statement in your MGT1500 portfolio. Check what that one covered before you start.
What you need to write — 300–400 words covering all six
  • Day-in-the-life
  • What interests you
  • What keeps you motivated
  • Your fit — with evidence, not adjectives
  • Your gaps, and how you would close them
  • The field's contribution to society
Citations required. Pull the day-in-the-life and outlook facts from O*NET (onetonline.org) and the Bureau of Labor Statistics — real pages you open yourself. Never take a statistic from an AI.
