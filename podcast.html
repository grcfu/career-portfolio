Podcast

A published episode on where software engineering is heading — and what that means for people trying to enter it.

Highest-value item on the list — and no citations required
  • Must be a published episode, solo or interview, on a business-education or industry-trend topic.
  • Start early. Distribution can take a day or two to go live — this is the only item with a delay you do not control.
  • Record on your phone or Zoom. Trim in GarageBand or Descript (both free).
  • Publish on Spotify for Creators (free) to get a public episode URL, then paste the embed below.
  • Write the script yourself. A podcast script is portfolio content — the Level 2 policy applies to it.
